Q3 Growth Proposal

Prepared by Avery Chen·June 18, 2026·6 min read

This proposal outlines a focused plan to accelerate qualified pipeline through the third quarter, concentrating investment on the three channels that delivered the strongest return in H1. The recommendation is deliberately narrow: do fewer things, measured weekly, with a clear owner for each motion.

1. Where we are today

Inbound demand grew 24% quarter-over-quarter, but conversion from trial to paid plateaued at 11%. The gap is not awareness — it is the activation moment. Most accounts that churn never complete a second session within their first week.

Recommendation

Shift 30% of paid-social spend into lifecycle activation, and instrument a single north-star activation event measured weekly.

2. The plan in three moves

  • Activation sprint. A two-week onboarding redesign targeting the first-session drop-off.
  • Lifecycle email. Five-touch sequence triggered by behavioral milestones, not calendar days.
  • Channel consolidation. Pause the two lowest-ROI channels and reinvest in search and partnerships.

Each move has a named owner, a weekly metric, and a clear stop condition. We will review progress every Friday and cut anything that is not moving the activation rate after three weeks.
